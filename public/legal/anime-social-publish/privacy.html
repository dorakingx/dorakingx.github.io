Anime Social Publish — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Anime Social Publish is a personal tool operated by Doraking (@dorakingx) that posts the operator's own content to the operator's own accounts. It has no end users other than the operator.

Data accessed

  • From TikTok (via Login Kit and the Content Posting API): the operator's open ID, basic profile/creator information (such as username and available privacy options) and the status of posts the tool creates.
  • OAuth tokens needed to call the official APIs.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to verify the operator's own account and to publish and check the operator's own posts.
  • Access tokens are kept only in memory while a job runs. Refresh tokens are stored encrypted and never published. Credentials are never shared or sold.
  • No data about any other person is collected. No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

Deletion

The operator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's app permissions settings, after which stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

プライバシーポリシー（日本語）

本ツールは運用者本人のアカウントにのみ接続し、本人確認と本人の投稿の作成・状態確認のためだけに TikTok の open ID、基本プロフィール／Creator情報、投稿状態、OAuthトークンを扱います。アクセストークンは実行中のメモリ内のみで使用し、リフレッシュトークンは暗号化して保存し公開しません。第三者のデータ収集、分析・広告・トラッキングは行いません。TikTokのアプリ権限設定からいつでも連携を解除できます。
