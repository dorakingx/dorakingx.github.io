Anime Social Publish — Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-09-30

What this is

Anime Social Publish is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Doraking (@dorakingx). It publishes the operator's own AI-generated anime illustrations to the operator's own social media accounts (including TikTok account @doraking_jp) using each platform's official APIs. It is not offered as a service to the public, and no other users can sign up or connect accounts.

Use

  • Only the operator's own accounts are connected.
  • Content is posted only after the operator explicitly approves it. AI-generated content is labeled as AI-generated where the platform supports it.
  • The tool follows the TikTok Terms of Service, TikTok Developer Terms and the policies of each platform.
